What is Tonk! Cricket?

A 3D cricket game for Android phones, made by one person. You bat and bowl in T10, T20, one-day and Test matches, play a career from school cricket at 15 to a Test cap, or run a team as its manager. It comes out on Google Play on 3 December 2026.

How do I bat and bowl?

There are three ways to bat, and you pick one. TAP is three buttons: leg side, off side or block. With SWIPE you swipe where you want the ball to go, or tap to block. AIM gives you a stick to point at the gap. All three share the same leaderboards, and you can change in Settings at any time.

When you bowl, you pick the delivery, aim at the pitch and time the release. With SWIPE you slide up from the BOWL button to aim, let go to run in, and flick to release. You can also set your own field: one tap for an attacking, balanced or defensive one, or SET FIELD to place each fielder yourself.

How big is it, and will it run on my phone?

About 10 MB. It needs Android 7.0 or newer and it is built to run on budget phones. Google Play shows the exact size before you install.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Matches, careers, the nets and the daily challenge all play without the internet, and the match-ticket timer keeps counting in airplane mode. The internet is only used to watch an advert, to buy something on Google Play, and to sign in to Google Play Games, which is optional.

Is it free?

It is free to start. Quick Match, Custom Match, the World Cup, a practice match playing the whole team, and the nets are always free, and simming a match never costs anything. Career matches use match tickets, and some modes use Play Now games. The Full Game is one purchase that removes adverts, tickets and the game limit, and adds custom careers. There are no subscriptions. Google Play shows the price in your own currency before you buy.

What are match tickets?

Every Pro Career match you play uses one ticket, whatever the format. You hold three, or two on Gentle. One comes back every hour, offline too, and an advert you choose to watch gets you one sooner. A match you have started will always finish: resuming it costs nothing more. A match you sim never costs a ticket, and Manager Career matches don’t use them.

What are Play Now games?

Six Smash, Super Over, Last Over, Bowl Them Out, Pass & Play and a challenge’s second try each use one game. A whole Pass & Play tournament counts as one game. You hold three. All three come back 30 minutes after you run out, or one advert gives you three at once. If no advert is ready, you play anyway.

What adverts are there?

Most are ones you choose: an advert for a match ticket, for three Play Now games, once a day to double the coins you earned that day, or two adverts to bring back a missions streak you broke. The game tells you the reward before it plays. Two short adverts play without asking: a drinks break at the end of an over in long matches, and an advert after some matches. Neither comes in your first three matches, and no advert ever plays during an over. There are no banners. The Full Game has no adverts at all.

Can I buy an advantage?

No. Purchases take away adverts and limits; they don’t make your batter hit harder or the other side worse. Coins can’t be bought with money, and stumps and helmets only change how things look.

Can I play with friends?

Yes, on one phone. Pass & Play takes two to eight people, in a single match or a whole tournament, with no internet.

You can also challenge a friend on another phone. After a scenario or a Last Over, tap CHALLENGE A FRIEND and send them the message. It has a code and the Google Play link, and they try to beat your score. If they don’t have the game yet, they install it from that link and your challenge opens for them. Playing a live match against a friend on another phone is not in the game yet.

What happens if a match is tied?

You play a Super Over: one over each, and the side with more runs wins. That goes for T10, T20 and one-day matches in a Pro Career, a World Cup or a Quick Match. A tied Test stays a tie, and so does a tie in Pass & Play.

Can I play as a woman?

Yes. A new Pro Career asks MAN OR WOMAN on the first step. A woman’s career is played in the women’s game: the women’s sides, their leagues and their World Cups, from school to the end. The Manager Career asks too.

Are the players real?

No. Every player name is a parody, and the game is not affiliated with or endorsed by any player, team, board or league.

Do I need an account? What if I change phones?

No account is needed. Your careers are saved on your phone. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional; it adds achievements, leaderboards and a cloud copy of your saves. You can also save a backup file from Settings and open it on your new phone. Purchases belong to your Google account and come back when you sign in on the new phone.

How old do I need to be?

The game is for players aged 13 and over.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Android comes first. The iPhone version comes later, and we will post the date on the What’s new page when there is one.

I pre-registered. Where are my DAY ONE stumps?

Open the game after it comes out, on the same Google account you pre-registered with, while you are online. They will be in the stump shop. The reward terms have the details.

I found a bug. How do I tell you?

In a match, open the pause card and tap REPORT A PROBLEM. The game makes a file about your game, and you choose whether to send it. You can also tell us on the Discord or by email.
