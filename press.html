About the game

In one line

Bat, bowl and build a cricket career. Dry humour, fully offline, tiny download.

Short description

Tonk! Cricket is a 3D cricket game for phones, made by one person. Bat, bowl and field, then live a whole career from school cricket at 15 to a Test cap, or run the whole side in the Manager Career. It is under 10 MB, plays offline and keeps a perfectly straight face throughout.

Long description

Tonk! Cricket is 3D cricket for phones. You bat, bowl and field, then live a whole career: school cricket at 15, club cricket at 16, the Under-19s, a provincial side, the A side, a franchise league and, if your form earns it, a Test cap. Or you run the whole side in the Manager Career.

Batting is about timing. Pick your side and press on time, or switch to Advanced batting and aim a stick at the gap you want, with modes to defend, loft or play something cheeky: the sweep, the reverse sweep, the scoop, the ramp, or the charge down the pitch. A marker shows where each ball will land, and its colour tells you its length. Bowlers bowl pace, finger spin or wrist spin: 19 deliveries in all, unlocked as they grow.

Matches run from T10 to full five-day Tests, with DRS, powerplays, weather, a pitch that wears, floodlit night games, lunch and tea, and day/night Tests with the pink ball. The career has debuts, rivals, press conferences, awards nights and a social feed that never raises its voice, in a world of hundreds of parody players who age, peak and retire around you.

It is free to start. Adverts only appear when a player chooses one for a reward, purchases are one-time, there are no subscriptions, and nothing you buy makes a batter hit harder or a result easier. Three extra grounds come with any purchase.

Features

  • Bat, bowl and field in 3D: two batting control schemes, and 19 deliveries across pace, finger spin and wrist spin.
  • A length-coloured pitch marker, with a colour-blind setting that adds shapes.
  • Pro Career: school at 15 to a Test cap, as a batter, bowler, all-rounder or wicketkeeper.
  • Manager Career: a club, a province, a franchise or a national side; the XI, wages, the franchise auction, a field editor, loans and legend XIs.
  • T10, T20, one-day matches and five-day Tests, with DRS, weather, a wearing pitch and a match clock.
  • Quick matches, party games, Pass & Play for two to eight people, nets, a daily challenge and weekly scenarios.
  • Four difficulties: Gentle, Tidy, Fiery and Brutal.
  • Under 10 MB, plays offline, runs on budget phones.
