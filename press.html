About the game

In one line

A cricket game for Android where you bat, bowl and play a career from school cricket to a Test cap.

Short description

Tonk! Cricket is a 3D cricket game for Android phones, made by one person. You bat and bowl in T10, T20, ODI and Test matches, play a Pro Career from school cricket at 15 to a Test cap, or pick the team in the Manager Career. It plays offline and comes out on Google Play on 3 December 2026.

Long description

Tonk! Cricket is 3D cricket for Android phones. There are two ways to bat. Simple batting has buttons for the leg side, the off side and the block, and your timing decides the shot. Advanced batting gives you a stick to aim at the gap, with Defend, Loft and Play, and a Cheeky mode for the sweep, the reverse sweep, the scoop, the ramp and the charge down the pitch. A marker shows where each ball will pitch, coloured by length. Bowlers bowl pace, finger spin or wrist spin and learn new deliveries as they improve.

The Pro Career starts at school at 15. From there it goes through club cricket, the Under-19s and the Under-19 World Cup, domestic cricket, the A side and franchise leagues to international cricket and a Test cap. Every tournament keeps its own record book with records a good player could actually break, and tapping a record you set opens the scorecard of that match. In the Manager Career you pick the XI, set the tactics and the field, agree contracts and bid at the franchise auction.

Matches run from T10 to five-day Tests, with DRS, weather, a wearing pitch, floodlit night games and day/night Tests with the pink ball. A field map shows every fielder by name. There are World Cups, Pass & Play for two to eight people on one phone, a daily challenge and the nets. Daily missions pay coins, which buy cosmetic stumps such as gold, neon, laser and flame. Coins can't be bought with money.

The game is free to start. After the first season, career matches use match tickets that refill one an hour. The Full Game is one purchase and removes adverts and tickets. There are no subscriptions, and nothing you buy makes you hit harder or win more easily.
