Tonk! Cricket

Pre-registration Reward Terms

Effective: 9 November 2026

These terms cover the reward for pre-registering for Tonk! Cricket ("the game") on Google Play. The game is made by Muppet Dev ("we", "us").

The reward

  • The reward is the DAY ONE stumps: a cosmetic stumps design for the game's matches. It changes how the stumps look and nothing else. It gives no advantage in play.
  • It is free. It can't be bought, sold, traded or exchanged for money, coins or anything else.

Who gets it

  • Anyone who pre-registers for the game on Google Play while pre-registration is open, in a country where pre-registration is offered.
  • Google Play delivers the reward to the Google account that pre-registered. It appears in the game the first time that account opens the game after installing it from Google Play, while connected to the internet.

Other terms

  • One reward per Google account.
  • The reward is kept with the game's saves. If the game is removed without a backup, the stumps return when the same Google account installs the game again from Google Play.
  • Google's own terms for pre-registration rewards also apply.
  • We may change these terms if the law or Google Play's rules require it. The date above shows the latest version.
